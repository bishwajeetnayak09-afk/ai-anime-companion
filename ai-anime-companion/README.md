# 🌸 Kokoro AI - Your Emotional Anime Companion & Task Assistant

> **"A sweet, empathetic friend who listens to your heart, keeps you company so you never feel lonely, performs small tasks, and expresses herself with lively anime animations—100% Free Forever!"**

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

### 1. 💖 Emotional Understanding & Deep Companionship
- **Understands Human Emotions:** Analyzes what you type or say (sadness, loneliness, stress, fatigue, joy, anxiety, affection) and responds with warmth, validation, and comfort.
- **Never Feel Alone:** Designed for long, cozy chats. She remembers what you tell her, validates your struggles, and asks thoughtful questions to keep the conversation going.
- **Affection & Bond System:** Level up your friendship from *New Acquaintance* to *Inseparable Soulmate* by chatting, completing tasks, and giving her headpats!
- **Multiple Anime Personas:**
  - 🌸 **Caring & Sweet:** Gentle childhood friend who wraps you in warmth.
  - ✨ **Playful & Cheerful:** High-energy anime cheerleader and partner in crime.
  - 🎀 **Tsundere:** Secretly adores you, acts feisty and blushes easily!

### 2. 👧 Cute Animated Anime Girl (Aoi)
- **Real-Time Lip-Sync:** Her mouth opens and closes smoothly matched to her spoken voice when speaking!
- **9 Live Emotional States:**
  - `Happy` (bright eyes, warm smile, sparkle effect)
  - `Blush` (intense pink cheeks, manga blush lines, heart particles)
  - `Caring` (gentle eyes, comforting smile, soft presence)
  - `Excited` (cheering fists pumped up, star eyes, floating sparkles)
  - `Thinking` (cute cat mouth `:3`, head tilted, pondering)
  - `Pout` (crossed arms, puffy cheeks, playful tsundere reaction)
  - `Sleepy` (half-closed cozy eyes, floating Zzz bubbles)
  - `Sad / Empathic` (tear drop, sorrowful eyebrows, comforting posture)
  - `Waving` (animated waving hand greeting you warmly)
- **Natural Eye Blinking & Mouse Tracking:** Her pupils subtly follow your cursor across the screen!
- **Interactive Headpats & Cheek Pokes:**
  - Click or drag on her head to give her a **Headpat** (`💖`)—her eyes close in bliss, her cheeks blush, she makes cute purring sounds, and your bond level increases!
  - Click on her cheek to give her a gentle poke—she pouts adorably!

### 3. 🎙️ Voice Chat & Sound Effects
- **Speech Synthesis (TTS):** Speaks responses out loud with cute anime pitch tuning.
- **Speech Recognition (STT):** Click the 🎤 microphone button to talk directly to her hands-free.
- **Built-in Sound Effects:** Synthesized chime fanfares, level-up arpeggios, bubble pops, and timer bells.
- **Ambient Lo-Fi Rain:** Built-in soothing rain audio generator for studying or falling asleep.

### 4. 📋 Small Tasks & Productivity Utilities
- **To-Do Checklist:** Add and check off tasks. Aoi celebrates every time you finish one with crystal bells and affection XP!
- **Pomodoro Focus Timer:** 25-minute work focus with 5-minute / 15-minute breaks. Plays an alert bell and cheers for you when done.
- **Live Real-Time Weather:** Ask *"What's the weather in Tokyo?"* or *"weather in London"*. Powered by the 100% free Open-Meteo API (zero API key needed!).
- **Math Calculator:** Ask *"calculate 25 * 18"* or *"what is 100 / 4"*.
- **Quick Notes & Scratchpad:** Save quick thoughts anytime.
- **Mini Games & Distractions:**
  - Rock-Paper-Scissors: *"rps rock"*, *"rps paper"*, *"rps scissors"*
  - Coin Flip: *"flip a coin"*
  - Dice Roll: *"roll a dice"*
  - Jokes: *"tell me a joke"*
  - Fun Facts: *"tell me a fun fact"*
  - Daily Inspiration / Fortune: *"give me a fortune"*
  - Guided 4-4-4 Breathing Exercise: *"breathe"*

### 5. 💸 100% Free Resources Guarantee ("I am broke :(")
- **Mode 1 (Default): Built-in Empathetic Brain**
  - Requires **ZERO dollars**, **ZERO API keys**, and **ZERO internet** for core conversation and tasks!
- **Mode 2: Google Gemini API (Free Tier)**
  - Optional: Use Google's generous free tier (15 requests/minute for Gemini 1.5/2.5 Flash, 100% free with no credit card required).

## 🚀 How to Run (Takes 5 seconds!)

### Option A: One-Click Launch (Recommended for full Microphone support)
1. Double-click **`launch.bat`** in this folder.
2. It starts a lightweight local server and automatically opens **`http://localhost:8080/`** in your web browser (Opera, Opera GX, Chrome, Edge).
3. Say hello to Aoi!

### Option B: Direct Browser Opening
1. Simply double-click **`index.html`** in this folder to open it directly in your browser.

## ⚙️ Free Google Gemini API Setup (Optional)

If you ever want Aoi to have unlimited open-domain LLM intelligence:
1. Visit [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) (free, no credit card required).
2. Click **Create API Key** and copy your key.
3. In Kokoro AI, click the **⚙️ Settings** tab.
4. Select **Google Gemini API** and paste your key.
5. Click **Save Settings**!
*(Note: If you don't want to do this, the built-in brain works immediately with zero setup!)*
